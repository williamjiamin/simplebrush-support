Simple Brush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Overview

Simple Brush is a drawing, painting and animation app for iPad. LIPAI WANG designed it so that you can use every feature without creating an account or giving us any personal information.

Your artwork and files stay on your device

Drawings, brushes you make, reference images, video trace frames and settings are stored only on your iPad, inside the app. They are not uploaded to LIPAI WANG or to anyone else. Photos, images and videos you choose from Photos or Files are read on your device only to do what you asked — for example to make a brush tip or a reference layer. When you choose Photos, iPadOS gives the app access only to the items you select.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, share or track personal data.
  • We do not use advertising, third-party analytics, crash-reporting services or accounts.
  • Simple Brush does not make network requests for its drawing features. Apple's App Store is contacted only when you load, buy or restore Simple Brush Pro.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never receive or store payment information.

Exports and sharing

When you export an image, timelapse or video, the file is saved where you choose or handed to the share sheet you open. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

Children's privacy

Because artwork and settings remain on the device and are not sent to us, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete any drawing from the Gallery, and deleting Simple Brush removes the data stored in the app on that device, subject to your device's normal backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.

Contact
