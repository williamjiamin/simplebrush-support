Frequently asked questions

Where are my drawings saved?
On your iPad, inside Simple Brush. Open the Gallery (top-left) to see and reopen them. Deleted drawings go to Recently Deleted first, where you can restore them.

Will my reference photos or the mannequin appear in my exports?
No. References, trace lines, video trace frames and the 3D mannequin stay on private layers that are excluded from exported images, timelapses and animation videos.

How do I make a brush from a photo?
Open the Brush library, tap +, choose New round brush, then "Choose tip from Photos". Auto cleans up the lighting, removes dust and centres the mark.

What does Simple Brush Pro include?
A one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks MP4 animation export, video tracing, the posable 3D mannequin, brushes from photos, photo-to-trace-lines and advanced filters. Everything you made stays yours whether or not you buy it.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I get it here?
Open any Pro feature and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
