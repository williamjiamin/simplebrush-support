Simple Brush

Draw, paint & animate on iPad.

Simple Brush is a painting and animation studio built for iPad and Apple Pencil. Start with 836 free brushes, real wet paint mixing, layers and selections, then bring your drawings to life frame by frame with onion skin.

Trace poses from a 3D mannequin that never shows up in your exports, turn a video into trace frames, turn a portrait photo into clean trace lines, and make a brush from any photo. Everything works offline, with no account and no ads.
